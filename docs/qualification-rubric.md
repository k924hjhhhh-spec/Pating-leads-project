# MAX 2 — Qualification and Scoring

## Score: 0–100
- Service-area fit: 25 points
- Project completeness: 20 points
- Likely job value: 25 points
- Timeline/urgency: 15 points
- Contactability: 15 points

## Labels
- HOT: 80–100
- WARM: 55–79
- COLD: 0–54

## Hard rules
- Outside Broward or Palm Beach: reject or route to future expansion queue.
- Missing all contact methods: cannot qualify.
- Missing service type or location: request missing information.
- No final price or contractor guarantee is allowed.
- An estimate is preliminary until contractor confirmation.
- A human review is required for borderline scores, unusual scope, safety concerns, or unclear property conditions.

## Output
MAX 2 returns:
1. score and label;
2. evidence for each scoring category;
3. missing information;
4. risk flags;
5. recommended next state;
6. whether human review is required.
