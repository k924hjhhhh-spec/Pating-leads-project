# Pricing assumptions

The estimate engine is intentionally configurable. No customer-facing rate is
hardcoded until the owner approves local business assumptions.

Required inputs:
- labor rate per square foot
- materials rate per square foot
- preparation multiplier
- access multiplier
- contingency multiplier

The output is a preliminary internal estimate only, with low confidence until
site review. RYAN 3 must show the assumptions used and must not present this
as a final quote or guaranteed price. Final values require owner approval after
the EIN/business setup and operating model are confirmed.
